About MTG Lookup

MTG Lookup is an independent, fan-made card search and price comparison tool for Magic: The Gathering.

What you'll find here

  • Card search with filters for card type and color, no search syntax to learn. Every printing's art shows side by side, each with its own price.
  • Prices from Mana Pool, Card Kingdom and TCGplayer in one place, for non-foil and foil, with a link to the printing on the vendor's site.
  • A random card button for deck ideas, with an optional color filter.
  • A keyword glossary with plain-English definitions of 89 keywords, and in-depth pages for keywords like flying and trample where you can step through a combat that shows the keyword at work.
  • Guides for newer players: an interactive tour of how to read a card, a combat simulator that walks through attacking and blocking, and the basics of Commander.

Where the data comes from

  • Card data and images come from Scryfall, the community-run card database. Scryfall is not affiliated with this site.
  • Pricescome from Mana Pool, Card Kingdom, and TCGplayer. Prices change often, sometimes several times a day, so always confirm on the vendor's page before you buy.

What this site is not

MTG Lookup is unofficial Fan Content permitted under the Wizards of the Coast Fan Content Policy. It is not approved or endorsed by Wizards. Portions of the materials used are property of Wizards of the Coast LLC.

The site does not sell cards, hold inventory, or process payments. Every purchase happens on the vendor's own site under their terms.
