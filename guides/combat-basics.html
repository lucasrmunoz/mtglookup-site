The steps of combat

Combat happens once in each of your turns, after your first main phase. It has five steps:

  1. Beginning of combat. A last chance to act before attackers are chosen.
  2. Declare attackers. You choose which creatures attack and whom they attack.
  3. Declare blockers. The defending player chooses which creatures block.
  4. Combat damage. Every creature in combat deals its damage at once.
  5. End of combat. Combat wraps up, then your second main phase begins.

Who can attack

Any untapped creature you have controlled continuously since the start of your turn can attack. A creature that just came under your control has “summoning sickness” and has to wait a turn, unless it has haste.

Attacking taps the creature, unless it has vigilance. A tapped creature can't block, so every attacker you send is one less blocker on your opponent's turn. In a game with several players, each attacker attacks one player, planeswalker or battle of your choice.

Who can block

The defending player can block with any untapped creature they control. Blocking doesn't tap it. Each blocker blocks one attacker, but several blockers can gang up on the same attacker. Attackers that nobody blocks deal their damage to whatever they attacked.

How damage works

  • Each creature deals damage equal to its power, and all of it happens at the same time.
  • A creature with damage equal to or greater than its toughness is destroyed. Damage that doesn't kill it stays marked until the end of the turn, then wears off.
  • An attacker blocked by several creatures divides its damage among them however its controller likes. Since late 2024 there is no damage assignment order to set first.
  • Creatures with first strike or double strike deal their damage in an extra damage step before everyone else.

Keywords that change combat

Many keywords exist to bend these rules. Each of these has its own page with a combat you can step through:

  • Flying limits who can block it.
  • Menace needs two or more blockers.
  • Trample pushes leftover damage through to the player.
  • Lifelink gains you life for the damage it deals.
  • Indestructible survives lethal damage.
  • Melee grows for each opponent you attack.
  • Deathtouch destroys any creature it damages.
  • First strike deals its damage before other creatures.
  • Haste attacks the turn it arrives.
  • Vigilance attacks without tapping.

The keyword glossary covers the rest, including double strike and reach.
