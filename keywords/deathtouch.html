Deathtouch

Any amount of damage a source with deathtouch deals to a creature is enough to destroy it.

Keyword glossary › Deathtouch

What it does

A creature dealt damage by a source with deathtouch is destroyed, even if the damage is less than its toughness. One point of damage is enough. This works on any damage, in combat or not.

That makes a small deathtouch creature a strong blocker. Your opponent has to think twice before attacking into it with a big creature, because a 1/1 can trade with a 10/10.

When a deathtouch creature is blocked by several creatures, it only needs to assign 1 damage to each blocker for that damage to count as lethal. Spread out, its damage can kill several creatures at once.

Common mistakes

  • Thinking deathtouch affects players. Damage to a player is just damage; deathtouch only destroys creatures.
  • Expecting it to beat indestructible. An indestructible creature ignores the destroy, so it survives.
  • Assigning all the damage to one blocker. One point is lethal, so split it to destroy more of them.
  • Forgetting the damage has to be dealt. If the damage is prevented, deathtouch does nothing.

Interactions worth knowing

  • Deathtouch plus trample lets an attacker assign just 1 damage to each blocker and trample the rest over to the player.
  • First strike plus deathtouch kills a blocker before it can deal its damage back, so the deathtouch creature survives.
  • Deathtouch works on noncombat damage too, so a creature with deathtouch and an ability that deals damage, like “deals 1 damage to target creature”, destroys what it hits.
  • Fight effects make two creatures deal damage to each other. A deathtouch creature that fights destroys the other creature, whatever its size.
