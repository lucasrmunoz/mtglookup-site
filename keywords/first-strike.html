First strike

A creature with first strike deals its combat damage before creatures without first strike.

Keyword glossary › First strike

What it does

When any creature in combat has first strike or double strike, combat gets an extra damage step. In the first one, only creatures with first strike or double strike deal damage. Then, in the regular damage step, everyone else deals theirs.

If the first-strike damage destroys a creature, that creature is gone before the regular step and never deals its damage. That is how a first striker wins fights against creatures its own size.

First strike works on both sides of combat: it helps an attacker against blockers and a blocker against attackers.

Common mistakes

  • Thinking first strike deals damage twice. That is double strike; first strike deals damage once, just earlier.
  • Expecting it to save the creature against a bigger blocker. If the first hit doesn't kill, the blocker hits back as usual.
  • Forgetting both sides can have it. Two first strikers deal damage at the same time, in the first step.
  • Giving first strike after damage. It has to be on the creature before the first-strike damage step starts.

Interactions worth knowing

  • Deathtouch plus first strike is very hard to block or attack into: any damage kills, and it lands before the other creature can deal any back.
  • Double strike includes first strike, and the creature deals damage again in the regular step.
  • Removing first strike from a creature after it has dealt its first-strike damage doesn't let it deal damage again in the regular step.
  • Damage from the first-strike step stays marked, so a creature hurt in that step can die from the rest in the regular step.
