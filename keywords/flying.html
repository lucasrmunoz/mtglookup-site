Flying

A creature with flying can only be blocked by creatures with flying or reach.

Keyword glossary › Flying

What it does

Flying is an evasion keyword. When a creature with flying attacks, the defending player can only block it with creatures that also have flying or that have reach. Any other creature simply can't get in the way, no matter how big it is.

A creature with flying can block creatures with or without flying. The restriction only applies to who can block it, not who it can block. So a flier on defense is just a normal blocker that happens to be hard to block on offense.

Flying does nothing outside combat. It doesn't protect the creature from spells, abilities, or damage from sources other than combat.

Common mistakes

  • Thinking flying creatures can't be blocked at all. Reach and other fliers both stop them.
  • Thinking a flier can't block a ground creature. It can.
  • Treating "can't be blocked except by creatures with flying" as flying. That wording is a different ability, and reach doesn't get around it.

Interactions worth knowing

  • Reach is the direct answer to flying. Many green creatures have it for exactly this reason.
  • Effects that take flying away, or that stop your opponents' creatures from blocking fliers, turn a team of fliers into an unblockable one.
  • Flying is most common in white and blue, and shows up on most dragons, angels and birds in every color.
