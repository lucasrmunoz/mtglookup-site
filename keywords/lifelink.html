Lifelink

Damage dealt by a source with lifelink also makes its controller gain that much life.

Keyword glossary › Lifelink

What it does

Whenever a source with lifelink deals damage, its controller gains that much life. It works on any damage the source deals: combat damage or not, to a creature, a player, a planeswalker or a battle.

Lifelink is not a triggered ability. The life is gained at the same moment the damage is dealt, so there is no window to respond, and it still works if the lifelink creature dies in the same combat.

Because the gain is simultaneous, lifelink can save you from dying. If you are at 2 life and take 3 combat damage while your lifelink creature deals 3 in the same damage step, you lose 3 and gain 3 together and end at 2. The game only checks for 0 life after all of it is done.

Common mistakes

  • Counting only what the blocker "needed". A 5/5 lifelinker blocked by a 1/1 deals all 5 to it, so you gain 5.
  • Stacking it. A creature with two instances of lifelink still gains you life only once per damage.
  • Expecting life from prevented damage. If the damage is prevented, none was dealt, so you gain nothing.

Interactions worth knowing

  • Double strike deals combat damage twice, so a lifelinker with double strike gains you life twice.
  • Deathtouch plus lifelink makes a creature that is both a great blocker and a life swing.
  • Pump spells raise the damage and with it the life gained, which makes lifelinkers good targets for auras and equipment.
  • Effects that make a player “lose life” are not damage, so lifelink does nothing with them.
