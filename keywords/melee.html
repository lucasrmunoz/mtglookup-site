Melee

Whenever a creature with melee attacks, it gets +1/+1 until end of turn for each opponent you attacked with a creature this combat.

Keyword glossary › Melee

What it does

Melee is a triggered ability built for multiplayer games. When the creature attacks, it counts how many different opponents you attacked with creatures this combat, and it gets +1/+1 for each one until end of turn.

The count is opponents, not creatures. Ten creatures attacking one player still gives +1/+1. Spreading attacks across the table is what makes melee grow, which suits Commander, where you often have three opponents.

The trigger resolves after attackers are declared and before blockers, so the defending players decide how to block while looking at the bigger creature. In a two-player game there is only one opponent, so melee is simply +1/+1 whenever the creature attacks.

Common mistakes

  • Counting attacking creatures instead of opponents.
  • Counting a planeswalker you attacked as its controller. If you attacked the planeswalker but not that player, the player doesn't count.
  • Expecting the bonus on defense or on later turns. It only triggers on attack and lasts until end of turn.

Interactions worth knowing

  • Trample lets the extra power from melee get through even when the creature is chump blocked.
  • First strike on a melee creature means its bigger power often kills a blocker before it can hit back.
  • Each instance of melee triggers separately, so two instances give twice the bonus.
