Menace

A creature with menace can't be blocked except by two or more creatures.

Keyword glossary › Menace

What it does

Menace is an evasion keyword. The defending player must block a menace creature with at least two creatures or not block it at all. Three or more blockers are fine too, and the blockers don't need any special ability.

That makes a menace creature hard to block profitably. A single big blocker that could beat it isn't allowed to try, and two blockers means risking two creatures to stop one.

Menace is only checked when blockers are declared. If one of two blockers leaves the battlefield afterwards, the attacker stays blocked by the other. From there it is an ordinary multiple block: the attacker divides its damage among the blockers however its controller likes.

Common mistakes

  • Treating menace as unblockable. Two or more creatures can block it.
  • Thinking a menace creature can't block. Menace only limits how it gets blocked; it blocks normally.
  • Unblocking it when one of the two blockers dies early. The attacker stays blocked by the survivor.

Interactions worth knowing

  • Flying plus menace needs two blockers that can each block a flier: two creatures with flying or reach.
  • Trample plus menace forces a double block, and the damage left after killing both blockers still goes through.
  • Deathtouch plus menace is brutal: the attacker kills both blockers with as little as 2 power.
