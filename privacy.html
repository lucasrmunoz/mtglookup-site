What this site is

MTG Lookup is a free card search for Magic: The Gathering. There are no accounts, no sign-up and no server of ours behind it: the page runs entirely in your browser and talks directly to the services named below.

What we store

Nothing about you. The Commander game tracker keeps its state (players, life totals, counters, turn) in your browser's local storage so a page reload does not lose the game. That data never leaves your device and you can clear it from the game page or by clearing site data in your browser.

Who else sees requests

  • Scryfall supplies card data, images and TCGplayer prices. Your browser requests them from Scryfall directly, so Scryfall sees your IP address as any website you visit does. See the Scryfall privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site files. GitHub may log the IP address of visitors to serve and secure the site. See the GitHub privacy statement.
  • Google AdSense serves the ads that keep the site free. Google and its partners use cookies and similar identifiers to show ads and measure them, which may include ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings and read how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent before any advertising cookie is set.

Cookies

The site itself sets no cookies. The only cookies come from Google's advertising described above, and only after consent where consent is required.
